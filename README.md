# Hormuz Data Center Specification

The [fire and smoke detection proposal](docs/fire-monitoring-proposal.md) specifies 1–3 combined smoke/heat detectors, placement for the rack room and contained hot aisle, a battery-backed fire panel, audible sounder/beacons and automatic cellular warning calls. It includes procurement and installation steps, commissioning checks and itemized costs in Iranian tomans, with an [editable estimate](docs/fire-monitoring-estimate.csv).

The [data center CCTV and facial entrance access proposal](docs/security-proposal.md) specifies four IP cameras, local 30-day recording and one facial recognition entrance terminal with secure door control and emergency release. It includes coverage assumptions, installation and acceptance checks, an itemized price table and an installed planning total in Iranian tomans. Its [editable estimate](docs/security-estimate.csv) separates supplier price references from planning allowances.

The [antistatic mosaic-floor finishing proposal](docs/antistatic-floor-proposal.md) specifies a bonded ESD epoxy system for the 18 m² rack room, with copper collectors, two grounding points, material quantities, an itemized budget in Iranian tomans, installation steps and commissioning tests. It explains the limits of loose graphite additives and includes an [editable material and installed-cost estimate](docs/antistatic-floor-estimate.csv).

The [50 kW battery UPS engineering proposal](docs/ups-proposal.md) includes a 15-minute design basis, automotive battery suitability review, battery sizing for five backup durations, three-phase online UPS selection, AC/DC board architecture, protection and cable calculations, commissioning methodology, and itemized planning budgets in Iranian tomans. Its [editable installed-cost estimate](docs/ups-estimate.csv), [battery sizing table](docs/ups-battery-sizing.csv), and [reproducible calculation and verification script](docs/ups-calculations.py) accompany the preliminary proposal. Final equipment selection requires a site survey and manufacturer battery discharge data.

The [50 kW datacenter electrical earthing proposal](docs/earthing-proposal.md) compares driven rods, a shallow ring with rods, and a copper-plate pit. It includes Persian price sources, itemized material and labor budgets in Iranian tomans, implementation procedures, and schedules for one owner with the required specialists. Its [editable financial estimate](docs/earthing-estimate.csv) is available as CSV.

The [four-rack temperature and humidity monitoring proposal](docs/rack-monitoring-proposal.md) includes the architecture, implementation procedure, one-person schedule, sourced hardware prices and an estimate in Iranian tomans. Its [itemized materials estimate](docs/rack-monitoring-estimate.csv) is available as CSV.

The [four-rack air-quality and airflow proposal](docs/airflow-proposal.md) reviews international guidance and specifies a ducted cold-supply/hot-return layout for a 4.5 m × 4 m room, with filters assembled from raw media. It includes a cost table in Iranian tomans, heat-load assumptions and a 50 kW sizing comparison. The [dimensioned drawing](docs/airflow-layout.svg) and [editable estimate](docs/airflow-estimate.csv) are available separately.

## 1. About US

### 1.1. En

Hormuz Data Center is operated by Rayka Dan Trade & Technology Company. Established in 2025, the company began its activities with a focus on developing IT infrastructure and providing data center services. Today, Hormuz operates as a small, specialized data center in Eilam Province, providing a reliable environment for hosting and maintaining the digital infrastructure of businesses and organizations. Our services focus on server and equipment hosting, providing a stable environment for computing and network infrastructure, reliable connectivity, and responsive technical support. By combining technical expertise with dependable infrastructure and solutions tailored to each organization’s needs, we help businesses in Eilam Province and across Iran build a reliable foundation for operating and expanding their digital services.

### 1.2. Fa

مرکز داده هرمز توسط شرکت فناوری و تجارت رایکا دان کاوشگر بهره‌برداری می‌شود. این مجموعه فعالیت خود را از سال 1404 با هدف توسعه زیرساخت‌های فناوری اطلاعات و ارائه خدمات مرکز داده مبتنی بر هوش‌مصنوعی  آغاز کرده و امروز به‌عنوان یک مرکز داده کوچک و تخصصی در استان ایلام، بستری مطمئن برای میزبانی و نگهداری زیرساخت‌های دیجیتال کسب‌وکارها و سازمان‌ها فراهم می‌کند. تمرکز ما بر ارائه خدمات میزبانی سرور و تجهیزات، تأمین فضای مناسب و پایدار برای استقرار زیرساخت‌های پردازشی و شبکه، برقراری ارتباطات پایدار و فراهم‌کردن پشتیبانی فنی پاسخ‌گو است. در مرکز داده هرمز تلاش می‌کنیم با تکیه بر دانش فنی، زیرساخت مناسب و رویکردی متناسب با نیاز هر مجموعه، امکان بهره‌برداری پایدار و مطمئن از سرویس‌های فناوری اطلاعات را فراهم کنیم و به کسب‌وکارهای استان ایلام و دیگر نقاط کشور کمک کنیم زیرساختی قابل اتکا برای توسعه خدمات دیجیتال خود در اختیار داشته باشند.
